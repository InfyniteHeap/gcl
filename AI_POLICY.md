# AI Policy

Using AI to contribute to this project is welcome! However, you must follow some extra policy below.

- **AI should not be used to generate comments when communicating with maintainers**. We expect comments on our projects to be written by humans. We may hide any comments that we believe are AI generated.
- If you're opening an issue, **use your own words to describe concrete problem.**
- If you're managed to open a PR, **carefully review changes by yourself in advance before committing them.** Since AI is possible to generate unsound contents, it's your responsibility to guarantee the quality of them. Changes without any human reviews will not be approved and merged by us.
- If you're opening a PR, **use your own words to explain the proposed changes.** This includes the PR description and responses to questions. Do not copy responses from the AI when replying to questions from maintainers.
- **Open issues and PRs by yourself, instead of autonomous agents.** We will close issues and PRs that we believe were created autonomously.

Portion of this policy is inspired from [uv's AI policy](https://github.com/astral-sh/.github/blob/main/AI_POLICY.md).
